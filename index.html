About Me

What I Do

I am an Information Technology student at Kisii University and an aspiring software developer with a heart for building clean, user-friendly solutions. Hailing from Sotik in Bomet County, Kenya, I focus on transforming everyday academic and community bottlenecks into intuitive web applications that genuinely save time and effort.

Community & Leadership

Technology is most impactful when shared. I immerse myself actively in developer ecosystems, including hackathons, tech DevDays, and the Google Developers Club. Passionate about student leadership, I advocate for inclusive peer learning, collaborative problem-solving, and helping comrades unlock their full potential in tech.

Hiking as a Philosophy

Outside of writing code, my favorite recharge is hiking scenic trails with my loved one. Climbing mountains has taught me my core engineering philosophy: complex challenges aren't resolved in one giant leap, but one deliberate, resilient step at a time—always keeping your sights set on higher ground.
